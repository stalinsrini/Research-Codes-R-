# R9 — World Bank DRR policy adoption and implementation

Study: Political Economy of Policy Based Lending for Disaster Risk Reduction in Indonesia, 2005–2025.

- [Interactive heatmap](R9_interactive_policy_heatmap.html): download the file and open it in a browser. GitHub's file viewer displays the HTML source rather than running the interactive report.
- [Detailed heatmap PDF](R9_policy_heatmap_with_details.pdf)

Revised 9 November 2026. Generated in R: 27 explicit/implicit DRR policy actions, 25 continuing reform episodes, and 37 target/actual/supporting milestone records. This selected inventory is not the complete World Bank portfolio.

Select a cell to inspect adoption evidence, delivered activities, target values, reported results, dates, sources and qualifications. Results due in 2026 are distinguished from outcomes observed through 2025. Shared pooling-fund stages are not independent delivered reforms. Reported outcomes do not establish isolated lending effects or losses avoided.

The November recheck incorporates the World Bank financial-sector DPL-series completion report dated 30 July 2025, alongside Indonesian policy instruments and other implementation reports.
